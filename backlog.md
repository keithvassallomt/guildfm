## Schedules

I'm thinking of creating a schedule feature. Here's how it will work:

1. We have a new Schedule page, that only admins or curators can see. 
2. Different schedules can be created. 
3. A horizontal interactive bar is displayed, starting at open time and ending at closing time as per settings. 
4. The user can drag stuff onto the bar:
4.1. Radio shows - these can be resized, i.e. how long the show runs for.
4.2. Playlists - these are fixed size, depending on the length of the playlist. 
5. A small note indicates that requests may change the timings a bit. 
6. A cover image is auto-generated for the schedule. 
7. Schedules can then be selected in the Now Playing page
7.1. First, we see the horizontal bar (in the same way as queue, playlists and radio) showing the available schedules. 
7.2. When a schedule is selected, the bar then shows the currently playing item, and the next items, with the tuner 'bar' moving along by time (if this is unclear, ask).
7.3. There some sort of back button to go back to the schedule list (to switch schedules).
