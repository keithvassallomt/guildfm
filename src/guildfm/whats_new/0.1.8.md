## Talk stations

GuildFM's DJs can now host a show together. On a talk station, two or more DJs chat, bicker and banter between the songs, cut each other off, and keep running jokes going all show, with the day's odd news, the weather and last night's football thrown in. The songs still play, and so do your requests.

## Fresh covers

Every station and line-up has a new cover, painted from its name and its music. No more offices with radios in them.

::: needs create_stations
## Make a talk station

Give a station two or more DJs with ElevenLabs voices, turn on **Mostly talk**, and tell it what the show is about. You choose how much of it is talk and say how the DJs get on, and each DJ can have pet peeves, soft spots and a running obsession for the others to poke at. **Hear them together** plays half a minute of them before you save. See [Talk stations](topic:talk-stations).
:::

## Skip the music in monitor mode

Trying out a DJ in monitor mode? Turn on **Skip music when ready** in the monitor bar, and each song stops as soon as the DJ's next words are ready. See [Monitor mode](topic:monitor-mode).
:::
