## Our first line-up

GuildFM now has its first line-up! Featuring 80's Flashback with Gemma, Freshly Pressed with Malik, The Golden Years with Terry, The Lunch Bunch with Debbie, and of course Xennials' Delight stays right where it was with DJ Guildbert.

## Fewer repeats

Heard the same songs a bit too often lately? Once a song has played, the stations now leave it alone for a week, whichever station played it and whichever version it was. Expect a lot more variety.

::: needs transport
## Music after hours

Pressing Play after closing time now plays for 4 hours, then the music stops by itself, so nothing plays to an empty office all night.
:::

::: needs change_settings
## How long after hours

You can change those 4 hours in Settings, under Playback hours. See [Playback hours](topic:playback-hours).
:::
