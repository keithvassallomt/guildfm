## Making a station, step by step

**New station** on the Radio page now asks one thing at a time, starting with what kind of station it is: just music, music with a DJ, or a talk show. Each kind only asks what it needs, so there's nothing greyed out to puzzle over, and **Next** always says what's missing. See [Making a station](topic:making-a-station).

## Each station has its own page

Tap a station on the Radio page to see it laid out a part at a time, each with **Change**. Changing one part saves just that part. To turn a station into a talk show, change its type and GuildFM walks you through the hosts and the show. Its cover, its own jingles and Delete are there too.

## Suggest how they get on

On a talk show, **Suggest one** has the AI write how the hosts get on, from their personalities. See [Talk stations](topic:talk-stations).
