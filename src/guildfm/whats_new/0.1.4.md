## Easier on Android phones

GuildFM now opens on Android phones without Chrome asking for permission to see the local network. It still only works on the office Wi-Fi, TJardinePrivate.

## When Spotify needs a break

If Spotify asks GuildFM to slow down, GuildFM now waits as long as it's told, and search tells you when it will work again. The music carries on meanwhile, with songs that have played before.

## Search scrolls on

Search shows 10 songs at a time. Scroll down and the next 10 appear by themselves.

## Renaming people

You can now rename someone on Admin, Users: their display name, the username they sign in with, or both. They stay signed in. If you change their username, let them know, as they'll need it next time they sign in. See [People and roles](topic:users).
:::
