The kiosk is a touchscreen in the office, standing upright. Anyone walking past can see what's playing, vote on the queue and add songs with a tap, without a phone or an account. It shows its own screen, made for touch, instead of the app.

## Set it up

1. On **Users**, tap **Add** and make an account for it, such as **Kiosk**. GuildFM shows a temporary password.
2. Change its role to **Kiosk**.
3. On the touchscreen, open GuildFM and sign in with that account and the temporary password, then choose a password for it.

It stays signed in, and shows the kiosk's screen from then on. To change how it looks, or how many songs it can add, see [Settings](topic:settings).

> Run the browser full screen on the touchscreen, so only the kiosk shows.

## What it shows

- **The song that's playing**, spinning on a record, with a thumbs up and a thumbs down either side.
- **Up next**: the queue as a wall of album covers, each with its thumbs. The kiosk's own requests say **Kiosk**.
- **Add a song**: opens the office favourites (the [Best Voted](topic:best-voted) playlist) and a search, typed on the kiosk's own keyboard.
- **A QR code** for guildfm.com, for anyone who'd rather request from their phone with their own name on it.

## How it plays fair

- **It takes turns like one person.** With equal votes, the kiosk's requests take turns with everyone else's, so it can't push ahead of anyone.
- **Places, then a break.** The kiosk has 8 places. Each song it adds takes one, and one that plays gives it back. Once they're all taken, it takes a 3-minute break with a countdown, when nobody can add or vote at it. After the break, all 8 are free again.
- **Undo, briefly.** A song can be taken back for 10 seconds after it's added. Nothing else can be removed at the kiosk.
- **Two votes each way.** Each tap on a thumb is a vote, up to two thumbs up and two thumbs down on each song. So on its own the kiosk can't take a request out of the queue: that takes a third thumbs down from someone else.

Its requests and votes count like everyone's, towards the order and towards Best Voted. The DJ says a song came from the kiosk rather than naming anyone.
