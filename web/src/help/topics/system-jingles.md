**Admin**, **Jingles** holds GuildFM's **system idents**: jingles any AI station can play in the pause between songs, as well as its own. AI picks plays only these.

![The Jingles page](shot:jingles)

- **Add MP3s** uploads your own, up to a minute each. They're evened out to the same loudness as the voices.
- **Generate jingles** makes them with AI. See [Making jingles with AI](topic:jingle-generator).
- The play button plays one on this device. **Remove** deletes it.

## About GuildFM

**About GuildFM** tells the AI what GuildFM is, who listens and how it should sound. The AI reads it when it writes system idents. Change it, then tap **Save**.

Each station's own idents are in its editor. See [A station's jingles](topic:station-jingles).
