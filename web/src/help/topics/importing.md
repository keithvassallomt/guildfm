1. In Spotify, open the playlist or album, then its **⋯** menu, **Share**, and **Copy link**.
2. In GuildFM, go to **Playlists** and tap **Import from Spotify**.
3. Paste the link, and tap **Import**.

![Importing from Spotify](shot:playlists-import)

GuildFM makes a playlist from it, with the same name, and opens it. It's a copy: changes made in Spotify afterwards don't show up in GuildFM.

## What can be imported

- **Any album.** It becomes a playlist named after the album, in track order.
- **A playlist** only if GuildFM's connected Spotify account owns it, or collaborates on it. Spotify doesn't let GuildFM read anyone else's playlists, including Spotify's own.

To import someone else's playlist, first copy its songs into one of the connected account's playlists in the Spotify app, then import that.

::: needs change_settings
The connected account is under **Connections**, in [Settings](topic:settings).
:::
::: lacks change_settings
An admin can tell you which Spotify account is connected.
:::

Songs Spotify can't play in the UK are left out, and so are explicit ones unless an admin allows them.
