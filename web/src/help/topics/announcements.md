Announcements are read out on the speakers in a natural-sounding voice. Use them for lunch, meetings, a parcel at the front desk or a fire alarm test. They can play once, straight away, or repeat on a schedule.

![The Announcements page](shot:announcements)

## Make an announcement

::: phone
1. Go to **Admin**, then tap **Announcements**.
:::
::: desktop
1. Go to **Announcements**, under **Admin** in the sidebar.
:::
2. Tap **New**.
3. Under **What should it say?**, type the message.
4. Tap **Preview** to hear it on this device. Nobody else hears a preview.
5. Under **Where**, leave **All rooms**, or tap the rooms it should play in. A room that's playing the TV is left out.

![Writing the message and picking rooms](shot:announcement-message)

6. Under **When**, choose **Now**, or **Schedule** to pick a day and a time.
7. Under **If music is playing**, choose whether it waits for the song to finish or plays over the music.
8. Read the line above the buttons. It sums up what will happen, such as "Plays in all rooms every Friday at 12:45, after the current song."
9. Tap **Play now**, or **Schedule**.

![Scheduling an announcement](shot:announcement-when)

### The voice

It's read out in the announcement voice chosen in **Settings**, which the editor names under the message.

With some voices, such as Guildbert, you can also **Add an emotion**, such as Excited or Whispers. It goes into the message where the cursor is, in square brackets, and changes how the words after it are said. Try it with **Preview**.

## Wait for the song, or play over the music?

- **Wait for the song to finish** is the usual choice. The song plays to its end, then the announcement, then the next song.
- **Play over the music** is for when it can't wait. The music goes quiet while the announcement plays, then comes back up.

If the editor says **There may be a slight echo between rooms**, that's because each room plays the announcement by itself, so they can be a fraction of a second apart. It happens over the music, or when you've picked more than one room.

## Repeating announcements

When you schedule one, **Repeat** sets how often it plays:

- **Just once**, on the day and at the time you picked.
- **Every day** or **Weekdays**, at that time.
- **Chosen days**: tap the days underneath.
- **Every hour**, at the same minutes past.

A repeating announcement starts on the day you pick and keeps going until you turn it off, unless you set the day it **Ends**.

Repeats keep to GuildFM's playback hours (in **Settings**), so "every day" means every day the music is on.

## Look after scheduled announcements

On the Announcements page, each one under **Scheduled** shows when and where it plays, and when it plays next.

![A scheduled announcement](shot:announcement-card)

- **Play now** plays it straight away, over the music, without changing its schedule.
- **Edit** opens it to change. **Delete announcement** is at the bottom of the editor.
- The switch pauses it, and turns it back on. A paused one stays on the list, marked **Paused**.

**Recently played** lists what's been read out lately, and any that didn't play, with why.

## When announcements don't play

- They don't play while GuildFM is stopped, or outside its playback hours. One that comes due then is skipped, not saved for later, and shows under **Recently played** as not played.
- They still play while the music is paused.
- They always go before the DJ.
- **Play now** works whenever GuildFM is playing or paused, even outside playback hours.

> **Tip:** For a quick message to every room, the **Announce** button on **Now Playing** is faster. See [A quick announcement](topic:quick-announcement).
