GuildFM works in any web browser, but you can also put it on your phone's home screen, or your computer's dock. It then opens in its own window, with its own icon.

## On an iPhone or iPad

1. Open GuildFM in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

## On Android or a computer

- If your browser offers to install GuildFM, your **Profile** has an **Install GuildFM** button, under **GuildFM as an app**. Tap it.
- If not, look in your browser's menu for **Add to Home screen** or **Install**.

![GuildFM as an app, on your Profile](shot:profile-install)

> **Good to know:** Some browsers only install apps from a secure (https) address. Where they won't, the icon they add opens GuildFM in a browser tab instead. It works just the same.

Once GuildFM is installed and open as an app, the **GuildFM as an app** section disappears from your Profile.
