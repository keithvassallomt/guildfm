Requests always play first. Between them, one source fills the gaps: a playlist, an AI radio station, or a line-up, which switches between them by the clock.

## Switch to another source

::: desktop
1. On **Now Playing**, find it in the **Playlists**, **Radio** or **Line-ups** row.
2. Click it.
:::
::: phone
1. On **Now Playing**, under **Sources**, tap the **Playlists**, **Radio** or **Line-ups** tab.
2. Tap the one you want.
:::

![Picking a playlist on Now Playing](shot:now-sources)

It takes over after the song that's playing now, and requests still play first. Until then its badge says **After this song**, and while requests are playing, **Fills the gaps**.

You can also put one on from the **Playlists**, **Radio** or **Line-ups** page. See [Playlists](topic:playlists), [AI radio stations](topic:radio) and [Line-ups](topic:lineups).

## Requests only

**Requests only**, with the queue, means nothing fills the gaps. After this song, only requests play, and the music stops when they run out.

![Requests only](shot:now-requests-only)

## When the music runs out

If a playlist plays to its end, or nothing was ever chosen, GuildFM goes by the **When the queue runs out** setting: start the playlist again, play a default station, or let the AI pick songs that follow on from what's been playing.

::: needs change_settings
It's in [Settings](topic:settings).
:::
::: lacks change_settings
An admin chooses which.
:::
