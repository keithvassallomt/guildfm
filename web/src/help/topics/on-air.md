Some of GuildFM's AI radio stations have a DJ: an AI voice with a name and a personality, who talks between songs. They introduce what's coming up, share a bit of trivia, mention the time or the weather, and give a shout-out when someone's request plays.

The DJ always talks between songs, never over one, so every room hears them at the same time.

## The DJ and your requests

When your request plays on a station with a DJ, the DJ might say your name, such as "This one's for Priya, who asked for some Fleetwood Mac." If it suits their personality, they may gently tease you for asking for the same artist again.

To keep your name off air, go to your **Profile** and turn off **Announce my requests**.

## Jingles

Jingles are short musical idents, like the ones between songs on the radio. AI stations play one now and then in the pause between songs. Playlists and requests don't get them.

## Announcements

Admins can make announcements on the speakers, such as "lunch is ready". They always go before the DJ, and play between songs or over the music.
