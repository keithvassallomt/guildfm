::: phone
Tap your photo or initials at the top of **Now Playing** to open your profile.
:::
::: desktop
Click **Profile** in the menu on the left.
:::

![Your profile](shot:profile)

## Your name

Your **Display name** is how you appear beside your requests, and what the DJ reads out when your request plays. To change it, edit it under **On air**, then tap **Save**. The same rule applies as when you signed up: your name, or what people call you, and nothing rude.

## Your photo

1. Tap **Add a photo** (or **Change photo**).
2. Choose a picture.
3. Drag it to move it, and pinch, scroll or use the **Zoom** slider until your face fills the circle.
4. Tap **Use this photo**.

![Fitting your photo into the circle](shot:profile-photo)

Your photo shows beside your requests, in the songs played today, and wherever else you're named. **Remove photo** goes back to your initials.

## Your name on air

When **Announce my requests** is on, the DJ on an AI radio station can say your name when it plays a song you asked for. Turn it off to keep your name off air. **How it sounds** shows an example.

![Announce my requests](shot:profile-on-air)

## Your requests

**Your requests** lists the songs you've asked for that haven't played yet, where each one is in the queue, and its score. **Request a song** takes you to the Queue.

## Your password and signing out

- To change your password, ask an admin to reset it. See [Creating an account and signing in](topic:signing-in).
- **Sign out** signs you out on this phone or computer.

Your profile also has the **Help**, and **GuildFM as an app** for putting GuildFM on your home screen. See [Installing GuildFM on your phone](topic:installing).
