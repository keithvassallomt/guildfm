1. Go to **Line-ups** and tap **New line-up**.
2. Give it a **Name** and a short description.
3. Fill the day with shows and playlists (below).
4. Tap **Create line-up**.

Once it's made, it gets a cover painted from its name and description, as stations do. **Paint another** paints a new one.

![The line-up editor](shot:lineup-editor)

The day runs from the earliest opening time to the latest closing time in the playback hours.

::: desktop
## Fill the day

The day runs across the screen.

- Drag a show or a playlist from **Add to the day** onto the day, or click one to put it in the first gap.
- Drag a slot to move it, or pull its ends to change its times.
- Click a slot to see its times and what happens at its edges. **Take off the day** removes it.
:::
::: phone
## Fill the day

The day runs down the screen, like a calendar.

- Tap **Add**, or a gap in the day. Choose **A show** or **A playlist**, which one, and its start and end times, then tap **Add**.
- Tap a slot to change its times. **Take off the day** removes it.
:::

![A slot's times](shot:lineup-slot)

- Everything goes in 15-minute steps, and slots can't overlap. Gaps are fine.
- A show can be as long as you like.
- A playlist's slot starts as long as the playlist. You can shorten it, but not stretch it.
- Under each slot, the editor says how the hand-over goes for a show, or how much of a playlist plays.

To change a line-up later, tap **Edit** on the Line-ups page, then **Save**. **Delete line-up** is at the bottom of the editor. Deleting a station or a playlist leaves a gap wherever it was on a line-up.
