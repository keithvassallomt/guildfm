There's one queue for the whole office. Anyone can ask for a song, and everyone votes on the queue with a thumbs up or a thumbs down. Songs people ask for (requests) always play before anything else, the most popular first.

![The Queue page](shot:queue)

## Request a song

1. Go to **Queue**.
2. Tap **Search for a song to request**.
3. Type the name of a song or an artist.
4. Tap **Add** beside the song you want.

![Searching for Fleetwood Mac](shot:queue-search)

Your song joins the queue straight away, and everyone sees it.

> **Tip:** Tap an artist under **Artists** to see only their songs. Tap the **×** beside their name to see everything again.

::: needs manage_queue
You can also queue a whole album. Albums show up in the search alongside songs and artists. Open one to see its songs, then tap **Queue all** (such as **Queue all 11 songs**). They go in as your requests, in the album's order, and don't count towards any limit.
:::

### Good to know

::: lacks change_settings
You can have up to five requests waiting at once, unless an admin has changed the number. The search shows how many you're using, such as **Your requests: 1 of 5**. Once one of yours has played, you can add another.
:::

::: needs change_settings
Admins can have as many requests waiting as they like.
:::

- A song that's already in the queue can't be added again. It says **In queue** instead of **Add**.
- A clock badge such as **Played 40 min ago** means the song played recently. You can still add it, though people may not want it again so soon.
- Some songs never show up: explicit ones (unless an admin has allowed them), and ones Spotify can't play in the UK.

## Vote

Each request has a thumbs up and a thumbs down. Tap one to vote, and tap it again to take your vote back. You get one vote per song, and yours shows filled in.

![Voting on a request](shot:queue-vote)

The number between the thumbs is the song's score: its thumbs up minus its thumbs down.

- The queue keeps itself in order, highest score first. With equal scores, the one asked for first goes first.
- A song that gets too many thumbs down is taken out of the queue. That's a score of -3, unless an admin has changed it.

## Up next is locked in

About 30 seconds before a song ends, GuildFM sends the next request to the speakers, so it starts on time. It moves up to **Up next**, with a padlock, and votes can't move it any more.

![The song up next, locked in](shot:queue-up-next)

## When nobody has requested anything

A playlist or an AI radio station keeps the music going between requests. The Queue shows its next three songs under the requests, after **Then from** and its name.

These songs have no thumbs up, since a playlist or a station keeps its own order, but you can give one a thumbs down. If enough people do (three, unless an admin has changed it), that song is skipped this time round. The count beside it, such as **1/3**, shows how close it is.

![The playlist's next songs](shot:queue-upcoming)

## Take a request back

1. Tap the small arrow at the right-hand end of your request.
2. Tap the **×** that slides out.

![Removing a request](shot:queue-remove)

::: lacks manage_queue
You can only remove your own requests.
:::

::: needs manage_queue
You can remove anyone's request the same way.

You can also move requests up and down the queue, whatever the votes say:

::: desktop
- Drag a request to where it should go.
:::
::: phone
- Press and hold a request, then drag it to where it should go.
:::
- Or use the up and down arrows that slide out beside the **×**.

A request you've moved says **placed by a curator**, and votes don't move it again.
:::

## See what's been happening

**Activity** lists the latest goings-on: who asked for what, who gave what a thumbs up, and songs taken out after too many thumbs down.

When your request plays on a station with a DJ, the DJ might say your name. See [The DJ, jingles and your name](topic:on-air).
