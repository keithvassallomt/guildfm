GuildFM plays music on the office's Sonos speakers, and everyone gets a say in what's on.

![Now Playing, the first page you see](shot:now-playing)

## How it decides what plays

- **Requests come first.** Anyone can ask for a song. Everyone votes on the queue with a thumbs up or a thumbs down, and the most popular request plays next.
- **Something fills the gaps between requests.** That's a playlist, an AI radio station, or a line-up (a plan for the day that switches between them by the clock). Curators and admins choose which.
- **Some stations have a DJ.** An AI radio station can have a DJ, an AI voice with a name and a personality, who talks between songs.
- **Admins make announcements.** Such as "lunch is ready", read out on the speakers.

## Who can do what

There are three kinds of account, and everyone starts as a user.

- **Users** request songs, vote, and take back their own requests.
- **Curators** can also play, pause and skip, choose what fills the gaps, look after the queue, and make playlists, AI radio stations and line-ups.
- **Admins** can also set the volume, stop the music, make announcements, look after people's accounts, and change the settings.

If you need to do more, ask an admin to change your account.

## Getting help

Tap **?** at the top of any page for help with that page. This help only shows what your account can do.

All of the help is under **Help**: at the bottom of the menu on a computer, and on your **Profile** on a phone.
