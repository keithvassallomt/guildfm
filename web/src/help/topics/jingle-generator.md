The jingle generator writes music to order. You describe each jingle, or have the AI describe it, and ElevenLabs makes them. You then listen, and keep or turn down each one.

::: lacks change_settings
It's in each station's editor, under **Jingles**.
:::
::: needs change_settings
It's in each station's editor, under **Jingles**, for that station's own idents, and on **Admin**, **Jingles** for GuildFM's system idents.
:::

## Describe them

1. Tap **Generate jingles**.
2. Choose **How many**, up to six.
3. Describe each one in its box: the style, the instruments, and any words to be sung, in quotes. Or tap **AI** on a card to have the AI write it. With words already in the box, it builds on them. **Write the empty ones** fills in every empty card at once.
4. Set each one's **Length**, from 5 to 15 seconds, and turn on **Instrumental** for one with no words at all.
5. Tap **Generate**.

![Describing jingles](shot:generator-describe)

The AI writes for the station from its name, description and DJ, as they are in the editor. For system idents, it reads **About GuildFM** instead. It also knows the jingles made here before, kept and turned down, so new ones come out different.

## Keep the best

Each jingle takes a minute or so to make. When one's ready:

1. Tap its play button to listen, on this device.
2. Change its name if you like.
3. Tap **Keep** to add it to the jingles straight away, **Turn down** to throw it away, or **Try again** for another go at the same description.

![Keeping or turning down jingles](shot:generator-review)

- If one can't be made, its card says why, with its description to change, by hand or with the AI, and try again.
- GuildFM checks the words can be heard, and makes a jingle again if they can't. If they still can't, its card says so.
- **Generate more** starts another round.
- **Done** closes it, and asks first if some are still to decide on. Closing it any other way keeps them for next time.

Each jingle uses ElevenLabs credits.
