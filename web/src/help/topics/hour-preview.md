1. In the station's editor, tap **Preview an hour**.
2. Wait about a minute, while the AI picks the songs and writes what the DJ says.

![An hour of Xennials' Delight](shot:hour-preview)

You see an hour of the station as the editor has it, saved or not: each song with the time it would start and why it was picked, the jingles, and what the DJ would say between songs.

- Tap **Preview** on a DJ line to hear it in the DJ's voice.
- Tap **Listen** on a jingle to hear it.
- **Try another hour** asks again.

Nothing here is kept, and real requests would change the real hour.
