Playlists keep the music going between requests. **Playlists** lists them all, with Best Voted first.

![The Playlists page](shot:playlists)

## Put one on

Tap the play button beside it, or open it and tap **Play**. It takes over from the next song, and requests still play first.

The playlist that's on says **On air**. **Take off**, at the top, takes it off: after this song, only requests play.

A playlist you switch away from remembers where it was, and carries on from there next time. Once it's played to the end, it starts again from the top.

## Make a playlist

1. On **Playlists**, tap **New playlist**.
2. Give it a name, and tap **Create**.
3. Tap **Add songs**, search for songs, and tap **Add** beside each one you want.

![A playlist](shot:playlist)

## Add songs and albums

**Add songs** searches Spotify for songs, artists and albums:

- Tap **Add** beside a song to add it.
- Tap an artist to see only their songs.
- Open an album to see its songs. Add the ones you want, or all of them with **Add all** (such as **Add all 11 songs**).

![Adding songs to a playlist](shot:playlist-add)

Explicit songs (unless an admin allows them) and songs Spotify can't play in the UK are left out.

## Change a playlist

- **Rename**, at the top, changes its name.
- The **×** beside a song takes it out.
- **Shuffle** plays it in a random order. It's off for a new playlist.
- **Delete this playlist**, at the bottom, deletes it for good.

While a playlist is on, the Queue shows its next three songs, and anyone can give one a thumbs down to skip it this time round.

You can also bring playlists and albums over from Spotify. See [Importing from Spotify](topic:importing).
