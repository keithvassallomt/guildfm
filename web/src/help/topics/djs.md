A DJ talks between songs on a station: introducing what's next, sharing a bit of trivia, mentioning the weather, giving shout-outs when requests play, and now and then saying how the office voted. The DJ only knows how many votes a song got, never who voted.

## Add a DJ

1. In the station's editor, turn on **Use DJs**.
2. Under **Choose a DJ**, pick a DJ from another station, or tap **New DJ**.
3. The DJ's card opens. For a new DJ, give them a **DJ name**, and describe their **Personality**: how they talk, their sense of humour, what they like to talk about.
4. Choose a **Voice**, and listen to it (below).
5. Save the station.

![A station's DJ](shot:station-dj)

Tap a DJ's name to open or close their card. **Take off this station**, at the bottom of the card, takes them off this station only.

## Several DJs

Add more under **Add a DJ**, up to six. On a music station only the first DJ talks between songs. Two or more DJs with ElevenLabs voices can make it a talk station, where they all talk, to each other as much as to the office: see [Talk stations](topic:talk-stations).

## Their hooks

Under the personality are three things that give the DJs on a talk station something to bring out in each other:

- **Pet peeves**: what the other DJs can wind them up about.
- **Soft spots**: what they go soft on, and give themselves away over.
- **Running obsession**: something they keep coming back to, show after show.

All three are optional, and music stations don't use them. Each comes up now and then, not every time they talk, and the first time one does, the DJs explain it, so nobody's left out of the joke. Each show on a talk station gets one or two of each DJ's hooks, different from the last show's, so the same ones don't come up every day.

![A DJ's hooks](shot:station-hooks)

## One DJ, several stations

A DJ can host any number of stations. Changing their name, personality, hooks or voice in one station's editor changes them on all of them, and their card says which other stations those are. How often they talk, what they say, and how they get on with other DJs are up to each station.

## The voice

Voices are listed under **OpenAI**, and under **ElevenLabs** for voices made especially for GuildFM, such as Guildbert. Of OpenAI's, Marin and Cedar sound the most natural. ElevenLabs voices can act things out, laughing or sighing in the right places, and they're the only ones a talk station uses.

To hear one, pick a phrase, such as **Track intro**, or **Your own words** to type your own, and tap **Preview**. It plays on this device, not the office speakers, in the DJ's personality.

![The voice, and hearing it](shot:station-voice)

## How often, and what they say

On a music station:

- **How often the DJ talks**: every so many songs, or every so many minutes. They always talk on the first song after the station comes on.
- **What the DJ says**: **Just announce the songs** keeps it short. **Add commentary** adds trivia, the weather, and the odd story about the song.

On a talk station, these are hidden: its talk and music slider says how much they talk, and its script prompt what about.

- **Announce requests**: the DJ says who asked for a song, unless they've turned that off on their Profile. With it on, the DJ announces every request, however often they'd normally talk.

![How often the DJ talks](shot:station-talk)

The DJ always talks between songs, never over one, so every room hears it at the same time. A line that isn't ready in time is left out. Announcements always go first.
