A DJ talks between songs on a station: introducing what's next, sharing a bit of trivia, mentioning the weather, giving shout-outs when requests play, and now and then saying how the office voted. The DJ only knows how many votes a song got, never who voted.

## Pick a DJ

On a **Music with a DJ** station, the **DJ** step shows the office's DJs as cards, each with their personality in a line and the other stations they host. Tap one to pick them, or **New DJ** to make one. **Hear** plays a line in their voice and personality, on this device, not the office speakers.

![Picking a DJ](shot:wizard-djs)

To change the DJ on a station that's already made, tap **Change** on its **DJ** card. A talk show picks its hosts the same way: see [Talk stations](topic:talk-stations).

## Make a new DJ

**New DJ** opens their details in place of the cards:

1. Give them a **Name**.
2. Describe their **Personality**: how they talk, their sense of humour, what they like to talk about.
3. Choose a **Voice**, and tap **Hear** to listen to it (below).
4. Tap **Add** (with their name). They're picked, and made when the station's saved.

![A new DJ](shot:dj-form)

## Change a DJ

On the card of a DJ who's on the station, **Change** opens their details. A DJ can host any number of stations, and a change to their name, personality, hooks or voice changes them on all of them, once this station's saved. Their details say which other stations those are. How often they talk, what they say, and how they get on with other DJs are up to each station.

## The voice

Voices made especially for GuildFM in **ElevenLabs** come first, such as Guildbert, each with what it sounds like. ElevenLabs voices can act things out, laughing or sighing in the right places, and they're the only ones a talk show uses. **OpenAI**'s voices follow, on a station with a DJ: they cost nothing extra, and Marin and Cedar sound the most natural.

**Hear** under the voices plays a line in that voice and the personality above, on this device.

## Their hooks

On a talk show, a DJ's details also ask for **Something to talk about**: three things that give the DJs something to bring out in each other.

- **Pet peeves**: what the other DJs can wind them up about.
- **Soft spots**: what they go soft on, and give themselves away over.
- **Running obsession**: something they keep coming back to, show after show.

All three are optional, and music stations don't use them. Each show uses one or two of each DJ's hooks, different from the last show's, so the same ones don't come up every day. The first time one does, the DJs explain it, so nobody's left out of the joke.

![A DJ's hooks](shot:dj-hooks)

## How often, and what they say

On a **Music with a DJ** station, the **Talking** step (or **Change** on its **Talking** card) says:

- **How often**: **Every song**, **Every few songs** (about every three), **Now and then** (about every 15 minutes), or **Let me choose** a number of songs or minutes. They always talk on the first song after the station comes on.
- **What they say**: **Just the songs** keeps it short. **Songs and stories** adds trivia, the weather, and the odd story about the song.
- **Something like** shows a line of what that sounds like, and **Hear** plays it in the DJ's voice.
- **Say who asked for a song**: the DJ says who asked for it, unless they've turned that off on their Profile. With it on, the DJ announces every request, however often they'd normally talk.

![How often the DJ talks](shot:station-talking)

The DJ always talks between songs, never over one, so every room hears it at the same time. A line that isn't ready in time is left out. Announcements always go first.

On a talk show, **The show** says how much they talk and what about instead.
