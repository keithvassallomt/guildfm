A line-up plans the day like a radio station's schedule: a morning show, singalongs at lunch, something quieter in the afternoon. Each slot is an AI station (a show) or a playlist, from one time to another. GuildFM switches between them by the clock, and requests still play first.

![The Line-ups page](shot:lineups)

## Put a line-up on

::: desktop
Click **Put on** on the **Line-ups** page, or pick it in the **Line-ups** row on Now Playing.
:::
::: phone
Tap **Put on** on the **Line-ups** page, or pick it on Now Playing, under the **Line-ups** tab. **Edit line-ups**, on that tab, opens the Line-ups page.
:::

It starts with whichever slot covers the time now, and stays on, day after day, until someone picks a playlist, a station, or **Requests only**.

Once it's on, Now Playing shows its slots along the day, with a needle at the time now: what's **On now**, and what's **Next**. The back arrow goes back to the list of line-ups.

![A line-up on Now Playing](shot:now-lineups)

## How the day runs

- Each slot starts at the first change of song after its time. A request can make it start up to a song late, but the day never drifts.
- When a show starts, it opens with its station ident, then its DJ says what's on and until when.
- A playlist still playing when its slot ends is cut off there. One that runs out before its slot ends starts again.
- In a gap between slots, the **When the queue runs out** setting decides what plays.
- On a day with shorter playback hours, the slots outside them don't play.
