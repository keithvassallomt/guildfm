Go to **Radio** and tap **New station**. GuildFM asks one thing at a time, starting with what kind of station it is, which decides what it asks after that.

![What kind of station?](shot:wizard-type)

1. **Type**: pick one of three.
   - **Just music**: songs the AI picks from your description. Nobody talks, so it costs no voice credits.
   - **Music with a DJ**: one DJ introduces the songs, shouts out requests and chats now and then. See [DJs](topic:djs).
   - **Talk show**: two or more DJs chat, bicker and banter, with songs in between. See [Talk stations](topic:talk-stations).
2. **Music**: give it a name, and describe the music: the styles, the era, the mood, and anything to avoid. The AI reads this every time it picks. **Try** fills in an example to start from.
3. For **Music with a DJ**, pick the **DJ** and say how they talk. For a **Talk show**, pick the **Hosts** and shape **The show**. **Just music** skips these.
4. **Extras**: the switches below.
5. **Review**: everything you chose, each with **Change** to go back to it. Tap **Create station**.

![Name and music](shot:wizard-music)

If something's missing, **Next** says what. **Back**, or the steps along the top on a wider screen, go back to a step you've been to. On a phone, the top says which step you're on. On a wider screen, **Your station** fills in beside the steps as you go. If you leave with anything filled in, GuildFM asks first.

![Checking it over](shot:wizard-review)

Once it's made:

- **Put it on now** switches to it.
- **Give it its own jingles** opens its page with the jingle generator. See [A station's jingles](topic:station-jingles).
- **Back to Radio** goes back to the list.

> **Tip:** On the review, try **Preview an hour** before you create it, to see what it would play. See [Previewing an hour](topic:hour-preview).

## The extras

- **Play jingles**: jingles in the pause between songs. See [A station's jingles](topic:station-jingles).
- **Crossfade between songs**: the speakers fade each song into the next, hiding the quiet at their starts and ends. Only while this station is on.
- **Knows what's going on**: the station takes the time of day, the weather where the office is, and music news (such as an artist's passing, or a song contest winner) into account, in its songs and in what the DJ says.

## Changing a station

Tap a station on the Radio page to open its page. Each part of the station has a card: its type, its name and music, its DJ or hosts, how the DJ talks or the show, and the extras. **Change** on a card opens just that part. **Save** saves it straight away, and **Cancel** leaves the station as it was.

![A station's page](shot:station-page)

![Changing how the DJ talks](shot:station-talking)

To change what kind of station it is, tap **Change** on **Type**:

- To **Music with a DJ**, GuildFM asks which DJ, then how they talk, then saves.
- To **Talk show**, it asks for the hosts, then the show. See [Talk stations](topic:talk-stations).
- To **Just music**, it saves straight away. Its DJs come off this station, but they're still there for others.

Picking the type it already has puts its DJs back as they were.

**Put it on** and **Preview an hour** are at the top of the page, or along the bottom on a phone. **Delete station** is at the end, under its jingles. If it's on when you delete it, nothing fills the gaps between requests until the queue runs out.

## The cover

Every station gets a cover, painted by AI from its name and description, a moment after it's made. Saving a new name or music paints a new one. **Paint another**, on its page, paints one now. It takes about half a minute.
