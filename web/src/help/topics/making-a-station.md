1. Go to **Radio** and tap **New AI station**.
2. Give it a **Name**.
3. Under **What should it play?**, describe the music: the styles, the era, the mood, and anything to avoid. The AI reads this every time it picks.
4. Set the switches (below), and a DJ if you'd like one. See [The DJ](topic:djs).
5. Tap **Create station**.

![Editing a station](shot:station-editor)

To change a station later, tap its name on the Radio page, then **Save**. **Delete station** is at the bottom. If it's on when you delete it, nothing fills the gaps between requests until the queue runs out.

## The switches

- **Global awareness**: the station takes the time of day, the weather where the office is, and music news (such as an artist's passing, or a song contest winner) into account, in its songs and in what the DJ says.
- **Crossfade between songs**: the speakers fade each song into the next, hiding the quiet at their starts and ends. Only while this station is on.
- **Play jingles**: jingles in the pause between songs. See [A station's jingles](topic:station-jingles).

![A station's name, description and switches](shot:station-switches)

## The cover

Every station gets a cover, painted by AI from its name and description, a moment after it's made. Saving a new name or description paints a new one. **Paint another** paints one now, from the name and description as they are in the editor, saved or not. It takes about half a minute.

![The cover, and Paint another](shot:station-cover)

> **Tip:** Try **Preview an hour** before you save, to see what the station would play. See [Previewing an hour](topic:hour-preview).

If you leave the editor with changes you haven't saved, GuildFM asks first.
