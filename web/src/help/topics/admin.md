::: phone
**Admin** is the last tab at the bottom of the screen. It lists the admin pages.
:::
::: desktop
The admin pages are under **Admin**, in the menu on the left.
:::

![The Admin page](shot:admin)

- **Announcements**: say something on the speakers, now or on a schedule. See [Making announcements](topic:announcements).
- **Jingles**: GuildFM's own jingles. See [GuildFM's jingles](topic:system-jingles).
- **Users**: people's accounts and what they can do. See [People and roles](topic:users).
- **Settings**: playback hours, voting rules, voices and connections. See [Settings](topic:settings).
- **Status**: whether the speakers and everything else GuildFM needs are working. See [The Status page](topic:status).

The playback controls, the volume and **Stop all** are on **Now Playing**. See [Play, pause and skip](topic:playing).
