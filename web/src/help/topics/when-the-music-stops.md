The badge at the top right of **Now Playing** and the **Queue** says what the music is doing:

- **On air**: playing.
- **Paused**: paused for now. Announcements still play.
- **Stopped**: stopped. Nothing plays, not even announcements, until someone presses Play.
- **Closed**: outside GuildFM's playback hours, such as at night or at the weekend.
- **Announcing**, **DJ talking** or **Jingle**: one of those is on.
- **Overridden**: someone is playing something else from the Sonos app.
- **Speakers offline**: GuildFM can't reach the speakers.
- **Won't start**: the speakers stop every song as soon as it starts.
- **Nothing playing**: GuildFM is on, with nothing to play.

## Outside playback hours

GuildFM plays during set hours, such as 08:00 to 18:00 on weekdays. It starts by itself at opening time, if there's anything to play, and stops at closing time. Requests made while it's closed wait in the queue until it opens.

::: needs transport
Pressing **Play** outside playback hours starts the music anyway. It then plays for the after-hours time set in Settings (4 hours to start with), then stops again by itself.
:::

## Someone's using the Sonos app

If someone plays something from the Sonos app or the Spotify app, GuildFM steps aside straight away, announcements included, and leaves the speakers alone. Now Playing shows what's on the speakers instead. If the speakers go back to playing GuildFM's queue, from the Sonos app say, GuildFM takes over again by itself after a few seconds.

![Someone playing the radio from the Sonos app](shot:now-takeover)

::: lacks transport
GuildFM carries on when a curator or an admin takes the speakers back. Requests still queue up in the meantime.
:::
::: needs transport
To take the speakers back, press **Play** on Now Playing. GuildFM carries on with what it was playing before.
:::

## Can't reach the speakers

If the speakers are switched off, or the network is down, the badge says **Speakers offline**. GuildFM keeps trying every few seconds, and requests still queue up in the meantime.

A room whose speaker is switched over to the TV just drops out. The other rooms carry on.

Sonos sometimes splits the whole group up by itself, which leaves every room but one silent. When that happens, GuildFM puts the silent rooms back with the others within about 10 seconds, as long as nothing else is playing in them. A room you take out of the group in the Sonos app, while the others stay together, stays out until you put it back, so it's free for the TV or to stay quiet.

## The speakers won't start songs

Sometimes the speakers stop each song a few seconds after it starts. That happens when they can't get songs from Spotify, and it usually sorts itself out within a few minutes.

A song that stops like that gets one more try, then GuildFM moves on to the next. If three different songs in a row won't start, the badge says **Won't start**. GuildFM then stops going through the queue and tries the same song every 30 seconds until the speakers play it, so it doesn't use up the queue while it waits. As it starts waiting, it says so over the speakers, in the announcement voice. It says that at most once an hour.

::: needs transport
If it goes on, open the Sonos app: it may say why the speakers can't play.
:::
