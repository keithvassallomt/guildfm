The badge at the top right of **Now Playing** and the **Queue** says what the music is doing:

- **On air**: playing.
- **Paused**: paused for now. Announcements still play.
- **Stopped**: stopped. Nothing plays, not even announcements, until someone presses Play.
- **Closed**: outside GuildFM's playback hours, such as at night or at the weekend.
- **Announcing**, **DJ talking** or **Jingle**: one of those is on.
- **Overridden**: someone is playing something else from the Sonos app.
- **Speakers offline**: GuildFM can't reach the speakers.
- **Nothing playing**: GuildFM is on, with nothing to play.

## Outside playback hours

GuildFM plays during set hours, such as 08:00 to 18:00 on weekdays. It starts by itself at opening time, if there's anything to play, and stops at closing time. Requests made while it's closed wait in the queue until it opens.

::: needs transport
Pressing **Play** outside playback hours starts the music anyway. It then plays until someone presses Stop, or the next closing time.
:::

## Someone's using the Sonos app

If someone plays something from the Sonos app or the Spotify app, GuildFM steps aside straight away, announcements included, and leaves the speakers alone. Now Playing shows what's on the speakers instead.

![Someone playing the radio from the Sonos app](shot:now-takeover)

::: lacks transport
GuildFM carries on when a curator or an admin takes the speakers back. Requests still queue up in the meantime.
:::
::: needs transport
To take the speakers back, press **Play** on Now Playing. GuildFM carries on with what it was playing before.
:::

## Can't reach the speakers

If the speakers are switched off, or the network is down, the badge says **Speakers offline**. GuildFM keeps trying every few seconds, and requests still queue up in the meantime.

A room whose speaker is switched over to the TV just drops out. The other rooms carry on.
