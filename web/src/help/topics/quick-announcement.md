1. On **Now Playing**, tap **Announce**, under the volume.
2. Type what it should say.
3. Choose **After this song** or **Over the music**.
4. Tap **Play now**.

![A quick announcement](shot:quick-announcement)

It plays once, in every room, in the announcement voice. **Preview** lets you hear it first, on this device.

To pick rooms or schedule it for later, tap **Pick rooms or schedule it**. It takes what you've typed to a new announcement. See [Making announcements](topic:announcements).
