Now Playing is the first page you see. It shows the song that's on, how far through it is, and where it came from.

![Now Playing](shot:now-playing)

## What's on

At the top is the song playing now, with its cover and a line saying where it came from:

- **On air from the queue**: someone requested it, and it says who.
- **On air from the library**: a playlist.
- **On air from the radio**: an AI radio station.
- **On air from the line-up**: a line-up.

While an announcement, the DJ or a jingle is on, GuildFM's logo takes the place of the cover, and the line says which, such as **The DJ is talking**.

The bar shows how far through the song it is. The badge at the top right says what the music is doing, such as **On air** or **Paused**. See [When the music stops](topic:when-the-music-stops).

## Where the music comes from

Requests always play first. Between them, a playlist, a radio station or a line-up fills the gaps.

::: desktop
Each kind has its own row: **Queue**, **Line-ups**, **Playlists** and **Radio**. Whatever is playing now is lit up, with a needle on it.
:::
::: phone
Under the song, **Sources** has a tab for each kind: **Queue**, **Playlists**, **Radio** and **Line-ups**. The one playing now has little moving bars beside its name.
:::

::: lacks switch_source
Curators and admins choose what fills the gaps.
:::
::: needs switch_source
To change it, see [Choosing what plays between requests](topic:sources).
:::

## Played today

Underneath, **Played today** lists every song since the office opened, newest first: when it played, and where it came from. That's who requested it (with its score), or the playlist or station. **Show more** goes further back.
