The playback controls are under the song on **Now Playing**.

![The playback controls](shot:now-controls)

- **Play** starts the music, carrying on with whatever was playing before.
- **Pause** pauses it. Announcements still play while it's paused.
- **Skip** moves on to the next song.
- **Previous** goes back a song in the first few seconds of one, and otherwise starts the song again. It's greyed out when going back would jump over a song that's already been sent to the speakers.

**Play** works at any time, even outside playback hours. The music then plays until someone presses Stop, or the next closing time.

::: lacks control_playback
Only admins change the volume or stop the music altogether.
:::

## Volume and Stop all

Admins also get:

- **Whole office**: the volume for every room at once.
- **Rooms**: tap it to set each room's own volume. It says how many rooms are playing together. A room playing the TV is marked **TV**, and one playing on its own is marked **Separate**.
- **Stop all**: stops everything, the DJ and announcements included. It lasts until someone presses Play, or the next opening time.
- **Announce**: a quick announcement in every room. See [A quick announcement](topic:quick-announcement).

![Room volumes and Stop all](shot:now-rooms)

## Quick controls

On every page but Now Playing, the round orange button in the bottom corner opens the quick controls: previous, play or pause, skip, **Stop all** and the **Whole office** volume. **All admin controls** goes to Now Playing.
