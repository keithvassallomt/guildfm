An AI radio station picks its own songs from a description, such as "80s and 90s hits" or "gentle acoustic for the morning", finds them on Spotify, and plays them between requests. It can have a DJ, who talks between songs, and play jingles.

![The Radio page](shot:radio)

## Put a station on

Tap **Switch to this station** under it, or pick it on Now Playing. It takes over from the next song, and requests still play first. The station that's on says **On air**, and **Take off**, at the top, takes it off.

The tags under each station say what it's like: its DJ, how often they talk, and whether it follows the weather and the news (**Global awareness**).

## AI picks

**AI picks** is built in. It follows on from whatever's been playing today, and plays GuildFM's own jingles. It can't be changed or deleted.

## How stations choose

- A station never plays a song that played recently, even one it picked before someone requested it.
- While a station is on, the Queue shows its next three songs, and enough thumbs down skip one. Votes never change the station's taste, though.
- If the AI can't pick (the internet's down, say), the station plays songs it's played before, so the music doesn't stop.
