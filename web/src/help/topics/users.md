**Users** lists everyone, with their role. People can also make their own accounts.

![The Users page](shot:users)

## Change someone's role

::: phone
1. Tap the arrow beside them.
2. Choose **User**, **Curator** or **Admin**.
:::
::: desktop
Choose **User**, **Curator** or **Admin** beside them.
:::

It takes effect straight away. See [What GuildFM is](topic:welcome) for what each role can do.

![Changing someone's role](shot:users-manage)

You can't change your own account. Another admin has to.

## Add someone

1. Tap **Add**.
2. Type their **Display name** and **Username**.
3. Tap **Create account**.
4. GuildFM shows a temporary password. Pass it on in person. They choose their own the first time they sign in.

![Adding someone](shot:users-add)

New accounts start as users.

## Reset a password

Tap **Reset password**, then **Reset**. GuildFM signs them out everywhere, and shows a temporary password to pass on. They choose a new one when they sign in.

## Delete an account

Tap **Delete**, then **Delete** again. They're signed out straight away, and their requests and votes go with them. It can't be undone.
