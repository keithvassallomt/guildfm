Monitor mode plays GuildFM in web browsers instead of on the Sonos speakers. It's for trying things out, such as a new station's DJ or an announcement, without the whole office hearing.

## Turn it on

1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Under **Monitor mode**, turn on **Play in a browser instead**.
3. Press **Play** on Now Playing.

![Monitor mode in Settings](shot:settings-monitor)

Turning it on stops the music on the speakers, and GuildFM waits for Play, then carries on from the same song.

## Listen

While it's on, admins see a **Monitor mode** bar at the top of every page. Tap **Listen here** to hear it in that browser. Any number of browsers can listen at once.

![The Monitor mode bar](shot:monitor-bar)

Songs play as beeps, each as long as the real song, with a different note for each. The DJ, jingles and announcements play for real. Everything else works as it does on the speakers: the queue, votes, stations, the DJ's timing and the playback hours.

## Turn it off

Turn the switch off again. The music stops, and GuildFM waits for Play, then goes back to the speakers. Until then it stays on, even if GuildFM restarts.
