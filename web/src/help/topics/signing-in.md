## Create an account

1. Open GuildFM in your phone's or computer's web browser. In the office, it's at **guildfm.local**.
2. Tap **Create account**.
3. Fill in your **Display name**, a **Username** and a **Password**.
4. Tap **Create account**.

![Creating an account](shot:welcome-create)

- Your **display name** is how everyone sees you, and what the DJ reads out when your request plays.
- Your **username** is what you sign in with. It can have letters, numbers, dots, dashes and underscores.
- Your **password** needs at least 8 characters.

You're signed in straight away, with a user account.

## Sign in

1. Open GuildFM.
2. Type your username and password.
3. Tap **Sign in**.

![The sign-in page](shot:welcome-sign-in)

GuildFM remembers you on that phone or computer for about six months.

## If an admin made your account

An admin can make an account for you, and give you a temporary password. Sign in with it, and GuildFM asks you to choose your own:

1. Type a new password, at least 8 characters, under **New password**.
2. Type it again under **Type it again**.
3. Tap **Save password**.

![Choosing your own password](shot:choose-password)

## Forgotten your password?

Ask an admin to reset it. They'll give you a temporary password, and you choose a new one when you sign in, as above. Resetting signs you out everywhere.

To sign out yourself, go to your **Profile** and tap **Sign out**.
